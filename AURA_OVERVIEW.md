## Executive Summary

**AURA Creative Learning Workshops** is a nationwide educational initiative created and led by **Faber-Castell Malaysia** (trusted heritage since 1761). The program is designed to equip primary and secondary school students (ages 7 to 17) with lifelong cognitive and visual memory techniques—moving away from stressful rote memorization toward structured, intuitive, and creative learning.

Alongside the workshops, students can enter the **AURA Student Olympiad**, a national challenge featuring **cash prizes up to RM2,500 per category**, national recognition, e-certificates, and MOE-approved PAJSK co-curricular marks.

---

## What is AURA?

Many students study tirelessly yet forget key information under exam pressure. **AURA** addresses this gap by teaching students **"how to learn"**. 

Through a blend of neuroscience-backed memory techniques, visual sketch-noting, mind mapping, and presentation skills, AURA empowers students to:
- Transform dense textbook chapters into high-retention visual summaries.
- Anchor facts into long-term memory using spaced recall and colour coding.
- Structure complex thoughts quickly to answer open-ended exam questions.
- Communicate and present their ideas clearly and confidently.

---

## Core Learning Philosophy & Methodology

AURA shifts students from passive memorization to active, creative synthesis through four pillars:

1. **Dual Coding & Visual Memory (Sketch Noting)**:  
   Combining concise text with simple icons, arrows, and visual structures forces the brain to process information semantically and visually, boosting recall speed.
2. **Spaced Recall & Active Retrieval**:  
   Reviewing material at systematically increasing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month) right before memory decay sets in, permanently locking knowledge into long-term storage.
3. **Structured Thinking Frameworks (Commander's List & Flow Mapping)**:  
   Breaking down multi-step problems and essay questions into organized hierarchies for deeper critical thinking and structured exam responses.
4. **Communication & Public Speaking**:  
   Teaching students how to explain their notes clearly, preparing them for higher-level challenges, interviews, and future leadership.

## The AURA Student Olympiad

The Olympiad is a multi-stage competitive journey evaluating students' mastery of memory, creative note design, critical thinking, and presentation.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Mission 1: Qualifying Stage (6 Apr – 19 Jul 2026)           │
│ • Submit handwritten creative note + Complete online quiz   │
│ • Open to all registered students                           │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (Top 100 per Category)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Semi-Final: The Advanced Challenge (8 – 22 Aug 2026)        │
│ • Advanced communication & structured thinking challenges   │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │ (Top 10 per Category)
                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Grand Finale: 2-Day Physical Showdown (30 Sep – 1 Oct 2026) │
│ • Klang Valley venue                                        │
│ • Live note creation, critical problem solving & pitching   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stage Details

1. **Mission 1 (Qualifying Stage)**: `6 April – 19 July 2026`  
   - Students read their category's assigned Mission 1 article.
   - Hand-draw a creative note summarizing the knowledge on A4/A3 paper.
   - Upload the note and complete the category-specific single-attempt online quiz.
   - **Top 100 scorers per category** qualify for the Semi-Finals.
2. **Semi-Final (The Advanced Challenge)**: `8 – 22 August 2026`  
   - Shortlisted candidates complete advanced assignments testing idea organization, synthesis, and communication.
   - **Top 10 per category** are invited to the Grand Finale.
3. **Grand Finale (The Ultimate Showdown)**: `30 September & 1 October 2026`  
   - A physical 2-day event held in the Klang Valley.
   - Live on-the-spot note creation, timed analytical tasks, and live presentations in front of an expert panel.

## Submission Rules & Guidelines

To ensure fair judging and academic integrity:

1. **Paper & Medium**: Must be **100% handwritten and hand-drawn** on **A4 or A3 size paper**.
2. **Language**: The submission must be written in **English**.
3. **No Digital Manipulation**: Submissions containing digital text, digital drawings, graphic software templates, or printed materials will be **disqualified**.
4. **File Format & Naming**:
   - Take a clear, well-lit top-down photo or high-resolution scan.
   - File format: PDF, JPEG, or PNG.
   - File naming convention: `YourFullName_SchoolName` (e.g., `AhmadAli_SMKDamansaraUtama.pdf`).
5. **Online Quiz Protocol**:
   - **Submit the creative note first**, then start the quiz.
   - Each participant is allowed only **ONE (1) quiz attempt**.
